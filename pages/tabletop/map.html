About

Tabletop RPG tools, per campaign. Character sheets and enemy sheets are stored on the server (a character can have a password). Dice are rolled by the server into a log shared by everyone on the page. The map has tokens, walls, doors, fog of war, distance measuring and a combat tracker (initiative, turn order, effect durations). The Dungeon Master HQ tab (enemies, campaigns, dungeon master password) is shown to the dungeon master only.
