    About and syntax

    Evaluates expressions, unit conversions and equations in the browser while you type. Enter stores the result in the history. History and variables are kept in this browser. Spaces inside a number are ignored (2 600 000 is 2600000).

    Arithmetic2 + 3 * 4, 2^10, (1 + 2) / 3, 7 mod 3, 5!, 2x means 2 times x
    Equationsx + 10 = 200, x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0, x^3 = 8, 2^x = 64, sqrt(x) = 3. Any equation with one unknown is solved, with complex answers where needed. When there is no exact method it is solved numerically.
    SystemsSeparate linear equations with commas: x + y = 10, x - y = 2
    Percentages15% of 80, 120 + 20% (adds 20% of 120), 200 - 15%
    Units5 km to miles, 90 km/h to m/s, 72 °F to °C, 2 h + 30 min, 3 GB to MiB. Length, mass, time, temperature, area, volume, speed, force, pressure, energy, power and data are included, with SI prefixes (k, M, m, µ ...).
    Functionssqrt, cbrt, root, abs, sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan, atan2, sinh, cosh, tanh, exp, ln, log, log2, log10, floor, ceil, round, trunc, sign, min, max, sum, mean, gcd, lcm, ncr, npr, hypot, mod
    Constantspi, e, tau, phi, inf, and ans for the previous answer
    Variablesa := 12 stores a value for later: a * 3. They are remembered on this device.
    Not includedCurrency conversion (it needs live exchange rates), inequalities with unknowns, equations that contain units, and symbolic answers like x = (y - 3) / 2.
