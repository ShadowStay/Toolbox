Frequently asked questions

Does this tool do anything?

It shows that Iprix is great. But you can also use it to share a nice message with friends or celebrate something special.

What file formats does the site support?

Images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, SVG, AVIF, BMP, ICO, TIFF, HEIC), PDF, Word (.docx), spreadsheets (XLSX, XLS, ODS, CSV), text and code files, MP3/WAV audio, and MP4/WebM video.

Can I process files without uploading them?

Yes. By default everything runs in your browser, so nothing leaves your device. In Options you can switch to server processing if you prefer.
