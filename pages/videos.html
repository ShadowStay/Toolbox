About

Streams the .mp4, .m4v, .webm and .mov files of the cloud storage (any folder) straight from the server, with seeking. Resumes where you stopped (kept in this browser), plays the next video automatically, and precaches the start of the next video, and of a video you hover over or focus, so it begins at once (needs HTTPS or localhost). Works with a mouse and keyboard, touch (double-tap the sides to skip 10 s) and a TV remote (arrow keys, Enter, Back, media keys). Add ?tv=1 to the address or use the TV mode button if your TV is not detected. Posters need ffmpeg on the server. Only formats the browser can decode will play.
