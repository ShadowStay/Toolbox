Omni File Viewer

About

Viewer, converter and merger. Opens images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF, SVG, AVIF, BMP, ICO; TIFF and HEIC when processing on the server), PDF, DOCX, spreadsheets (XLSX, XLS, ODS, CSV, TSV), text and code, audio and video (played by the browser). Exports: images to PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF or PDF; PDF pages to PNG or JPEG (one page, or a zip of all) or to text; DOCX to HTML, text or PDF (PDF through the print dialog, or LibreOffice on the server); spreadsheets to XLSX, ODS, CSV, TSV, JSON or HTML; text and code to TXT, HTML or PDF. Combine: images, PDFs and text files into one PDF; text, DOCX and PDF files into one text file; spreadsheets into one workbook. Limits: 30 files, 300 MB each. Files can also be opened from cloud storage.
